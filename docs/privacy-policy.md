# Privacy Policy — PokerStrike

**App ID:** com.carddefense.app
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-05

---

## Overview

PokerStrike is a singleplayer offline card defense game developed and published as an independent title. This privacy policy describes what information is and is not collected when you play.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

PokerStrike does **not** collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

- No account registration is required to play.
- No analytics or telemetry data is gathered.
- No gameplay statistics are sent to external servers.
- No advertising identifiers are used.
- No crash reports are automatically uploaded.
- No third-party tracking SDKs are included in the game.

---

## Steam Integration

PokerStrike integrates with the Steam platform via the Steamworks SDK for the following features:

- **Steam Achievements** — Achievement unlock data is stored by Valve on Steam servers and is subject to [Valve's Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).
- **Steam Cloud Save** — Save game files may be synchronized to Steam Cloud storage, which is managed by Valve. This data is limited to your in-game progress and contains no personally identifiable information.

All Steam-related data handling is governed by Valve's own privacy policy. We do not have access to or control over the data Steam collects in connection with your account.

---

## Local Data Storage

Save files and game configuration settings are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your machine except via Steam Cloud (if enabled). You may delete this data at any time by removing the relevant local files or disabling Steam Cloud sync for this game.

---

## Children's Privacy

PokerStrike is rated E (ESRB) and PEGI 3, suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children or any other users, as no data collection occurs within the game itself.
