## Short Description

Build your hand. Summon your units. Survive the wave. PokerStrike blends poker hand scoring with tower defense strategy — every combination you make becomes your defense.

## Full Description

**PokerStrike** is a card-driven tower defense game where the strength of your poker hand determines the power of your units. You are not just managing a tower — you are reading cards, making split-second hand decisions, and converting those decisions into battlefield results in real time.

At the start of each wave, you draw a hand of cards and choose your action: summon a unit whose power scales with your poker combination, swap cards to chase a better hand, or cast a spell to shift the odds. A pair gets you a unit on the field. A flush gets you something far more dangerous. The better your hand-reading, the stronger your defensive line becomes. No lengthy tutorial required — if you know poker, you know the fundamentals of PokerStrike within your first minute of play.

Between waves, choose from three reward options that push your strategy in different directions. Stack damage upgrades, branch into crowd control, or invest in economy that compounds over time. Every run builds a different deck identity, and the diverging paths mean that no two runs arrive at the same solution. With a session length designed around 20–30 minutes, PokerStrike fits a commute, a lunch break, or an evening cool-down without demanding more than you have.

Instant visual and audio feedback keeps every action readable. Projectiles connect, impact sounds land, and results hit the screen before you have time to second-guess yourself. The loop is deliberate: fast enough to feel snappy, strategic enough to feel earned.

---

## Key Features

- **Poker Hands Power Your Defense** — Pairs, straights, flushes, and full houses each map to specific summon effects and unit stat multipliers. Your card skill directly translates to battlefield capability.
- **Card Swap and Spell System** — Not happy with your hand? Swap cards before committing, or spend resources on spells that bypass the standard hand-to-unit pipeline entirely.
- **Wave-Based Strategy with Build Depth** — Three-choice reward system after every wave ensures each run develops a distinct build identity. Damage, control, and economy branches diverge meaningfully.
- **Designed for 20–30 Minute Sessions** — A complete run fits a short break. The pacing is tight, the feedback is immediate, and the replay motivation comes from chasing a better build, not a longer session.
- **Immediate Combat Feedback** — Projectile VFX, hit sparks, and responsive SFX make every card play feel decisive. You always know what happened and why.
